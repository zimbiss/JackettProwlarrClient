## 🚀 HOW TO USE THE VIDEO AGGREGATOR

### First Time Setup

1. **Install the APK**
   - Download from GitHub Actions (see above)
   - Enable "Unknown Sources" in Android settings
   - Install the APK

2. **Toggle to Video Mode**
   - Open the app
   - Look for the mode toggle buttons (circle icons)
   - Switch to **"Videos"** mode

3. **Add Video Sites**
   - Tap Settings (⚙️)
   - Go to "Manage Video Sites"
   - Add sites by URL or use presets

4. **Search Videos**
   - Enter your search query
   - Hit search
   - Results aggregated from all enabled sites!

## 🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING

**Q: I don't see video mode?**
- Look for circle toggle buttons at the top
- One button for Torrents, one for Videos
- Tap the Videos button

**Q: No results when searching videos?**
- Go to Settings → Manage Video Sites
- Make sure sites are enabled (toggle switches)
- Test individual sites with "Test" button

**Q: Videos won't download?**
- Grant storage permissions
- Check internet connection
- Some sites may be blocked in your region
